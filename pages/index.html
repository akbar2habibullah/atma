Method

Bound the read, compress the history.

Four global layers sit inside a 16-layer hybrid. Polar Attention separates what matched from how much matched; a gated-delta recurrent state supplies a fixed-size compression channel alongside the attention read.

Direction

Normalized content

A count-blind unit-vector channel aggregates signed value evidence without letting output norm grow with the number of keys.

Magnitude

Bounded participation

A participation-ratio statistic passes through an extreme-value-corrected null sink and bounded magnitude map.

Memory

Gated-delta state

A per-head recurrent fast-weight store compresses prior tokens into fixed-size state and is added as a third residual channel.

The important boundary

Synthetic retrieval is not real-text retrieval.

Aggregate scores conceal a sharp haystack split. At 256K, Polar retains substantial synthetic signal but nearly none on FinePDFs, and exact FinePDFs retrieval is zero.

Synthetic · 256K68.4%

teacher-forced token accuracy · 18.0% exact five-token accuracy

FinePDFs · 256K0.3%

teacher-forced token accuracy · 0.0% exact five-token accuracy

Other costs matter. Polar averages 43.29% on eight 2K zero-shot controls versus 45.15% for NoPE. At 128K, its attention decode is 16.3 ms/token on one L40S, while Atma-Raven-Titans records 2.27 ms/token with fixed recurrent state. The paper therefore reports a Pareto frontier, not general dominance.
