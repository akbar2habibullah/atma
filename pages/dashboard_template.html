Metric definitions and composite scores

Length-weighted metrics use weight L / 2048, emphasizing extrapolation endpoints. The performance index averages z-scores for clean perplexity, junk perplexity, and needle accuracy after flipping perplexity so higher is better. The efficiency index adds half a population-standardized MFU score. These composite indices are exploratory dashboard aids and are not paper headline metrics.
